## 3. Population reconciliations

**2,000 customer records → 1,991 active customers.** Not a data loss.

| Step | Count |
|---|---|
| Customer records in `customers.csv` | 2,000 |
| Appear in `orders.csv` | 1,999 |
| Have at least one Delivered/Processing order | 1,991 |
| Only Cancelled/Returned orders | 8 (4 Returned, 2 Cancelled, 2 both) |
| Never ordered | 1 |

1,991 is the revenue-eligible customer population used by RFM, CLV, and the churn
feature table. The 9 excluded customers are disclosed in the data-quality report.

**12,000 placed orders → 9,933 eligible orders.** 621 Processing are booked but not
fulfilled. Including them is a deliberate basis choice, stated in every figure; on a
Delivered-only basis revenue is ₹55.49 Cr, not ₹59.28 Cr.

**10.19% vs 11.61%.** The first is Returned ÷ all 12,000 placed orders. The second is
Returned ÷ (Delivered + Returned) = 1,223 ÷ 10,535, i.e. returns among orders that
reached a terminal state. Both are correct with their denominators named; the README
uses the first.

**53.97% vs 53.79%.** Headline margin is value-weighted: profit ÷ revenue = 53.9733%.
The discount curve uses the unweighted mean of line-level margin rates, which is
53.79% at 0% discount. Zero-discount aggregate margin is 56.67% and discounted
aggregate is 51.96% — the gap is product/order mix, not an arithmetic error.

## 7. What this repo does not claim

- Not real customer data. The generator is seeded; every number is illustrative.
- Not causal. Discount, churn, and CLV results are observational.
- Not a production ML system. No live inference, no monitored model, no campaign run.
- Not a general NL-to-SQL accuracy claim. 97.3% is 36 of 37 questions on one benchmark.
- Not a classic BCG matrix. It is a median-units vs median-margin proxy.
